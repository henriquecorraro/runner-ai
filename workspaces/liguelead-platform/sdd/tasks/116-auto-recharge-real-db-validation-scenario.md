## Expected Result
- Recurring processing creates payment, payment_item, and gateway_log records.
- Low-balance SMS debit triggers only the SMS low-balance rule.
- Voice and SMS Flash low-balance rules remain unprocessed when their balances do not cross thresholds.
- Declined gateway attempts do not create credit units.
- Scenario document lists exact ids generated by the local run.
